Markets Wrap: How US Stocks Are Reacting Ahead of the Fed Countdown

Market Overview

This week, US markets held steady as investors waited for the Federal Reserve’s policy announcement coming up on Wednesday, December 10. From Monday, December 2 to Friday, December 6, the S&P 500 hovered near record highs, the Dow eased slightly, and the Nasdaq stayed firm, supported by strong interest in tech and AI stocks.

Instead of reacting to short-term swings, investors focused on key data points: inflation trends, earnings guidance, and global market flows. Treasury yields were stable throughout the week, reinforcing expectations that the Fed may pause rate changes while leaving room for strategic adjustments later on.

Globally, ETFs continued to attract significant capital, reflecting sustained confidence across markets.

Executive Highlights

• S&P 500 near record highs (+16.4% YTD)

Stayed resilient with only minor weekly dips (~0.3%) as markets prepared for the Fed update.

• Dow Jones slightly softer (~0.4% weekly; +12.2% YTD)

Light pullbacks came mainly from cyclical and industrial sectors, taking profits.

• Nasdaq firm (+21.9% YTD)

Supported by strong performance from AI, cloud computing, and semiconductor stocks.

• 10-year US Treasury yield stable (~4.08% as of Dec 5)

Signals measured positioning and expectations of a steady Fed tone.

US Stock Performance

US stocks opened the week strong and mostly stayed steady. There was no panic or sudden trading rush. The S&P 500 rose a little early in the week before settling into a narrow range. The Nasdaq did better, helped by tech companies with good growth outlooks and continued excitement around AI. The Dow lagged slightly as investors took profits from industrial and energy stocks.

The main theme was clear: stability over noise. No big selloffs. No sudden swings. Just a market calmly waiting for the Fed’s next move.
