ABOUT PRODIGY

Why We Built Prodigy

Prodigy started with a commitment to practical technology education.

But the vision grew.

As we worked with more people and organizations, we began to see needs that went beyond training. Businesses needed better systems. Organizations needed to improve how they worked. And people needed the skills to build and work with the technology shaping the future.

That shaped the Prodigy we are building today.

We bring together software, automation, digital transformation, and practical technology education, creating an ecosystem where people, products, and organizations can grow together.

YOUR GLOBAL BUSINESS PARTNER

Prodigy is a technology partner for businesses and organizations navigating a changing digital world.

We help organizations improve how they operate, adopt better technology and build the capabilities needed for long-term growth.

Our work spans software products, enterprise solutions, automation, and professional technology education. Each part of Prodigy is designed to help businesses work more efficiently, make better decisions, and create greater value.

Today, Prodigy brings together three connected areas:

Technology Products

Software and digital products built to solve specific business and operational needs.

Enterprise Solutions

Technology, automation, and digital transformation support that helps organizations improve the way they work.

Prodigy Academy

Practical learning that helps individuals and teams develop the skills to build, implement, and work with technology.

Together, these areas allow us to support organizations from strategy and implementation to adoption and continuous development.
