HOW TRUECHECK WORKS

From the brand to the customer.

01. REGISTER YOUR PRODUCTS

The brand registers its genuine products with TrueCheck.

Each verification point is associated with the relevant product information.

02. APPLY THE VERIFICATION FEATURE

The TrueCheck verification feature is placed on the product or packaging.

03. PUT THE PRODUCT IN MARKET

The genuine product moves through the brand’s normal distribution and retail channels.

04. CUSTOMER SCANS

The customer purchases the product and scans the TrueCheck verification feature.

05. TRUECHECK VERIFIES

TrueCheck checks the scanned product against the information registered by the brand.

06. THE RESULT IS RETURNED

The customer receives the appropriate verification result.

Verified or flagged, the scan gives the customer a clearer answer about the product they are holding.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU GET STARTED?

What is TrueCheck?

TrueCheck is a product verification solution designed to help brands give customers a way to verify the authenticity of their products.

How does TrueCheck work?

A brand registers its genuine products with TrueCheck and places a TrueCheck verification feature on the product or packaging. Customers can scan the feature to receive a verification result.

What happens when a customer scans a product?

TrueCheck checks the product’s verification information against the data registered by the brand and returns the appropriate result.

What happens when a product is flagged?

A flagged product indicates that the verification result requires attention. The brand can use available verification information to investigate potential authenticity issues.

Can brands see where flagged products are being scanned?

TrueCheck can provide visibility into the locations associated with verification activity, helping brands identify regional patterns in flagged scans.

[CONFIRM EXACT LOCATION DATA AVAILABLE]

What products can use TrueCheck?

TrueCheck can be designed for products that are vulnerable to imitation or where authenticity is particularly important, including pharmaceuticals, supplements, cosmetics, food products, and other consumer goods.

Can TrueCheck prevent counterfeit products?

TrueCheck is designed to help brands and customers identify and verify genuine products. It should not be presented as a guarantee that counterfeit products cannot exist.

Who is TrueCheck for?

TrueCheck is designed for manufacturers, product brands, distributors, and other businesses that need a way to help customers verify genuine products.

Can customers use TrueCheck without technical knowledge?

The intended experience is simple: locate the TrueCheck verification feature, scan it, and receive a verification result.
