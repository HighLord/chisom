QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU GET STARTED?

What is WorkforceOS?

WorkforceOS is a workforce operating system designed to help organizations manage their people and workforce operations from hiring and onboarding through everyday work, attendance, payroll, compliance, and reporting.

Who is WorkforceOS for?

WorkforceOS is designed for organizations that want to manage their workforce from one connected system instead of relying on disconnected tools, spreadsheets, and manual processes.

What can I manage with WorkforceOS?

Depending on your organization’s setup, WorkforceOS can support hiring, onboarding, employee records, task management, attendance, leave, payroll, performance, compliance, and workforce reporting.

Can employees use WorkforceOS?

Yes. Employees have their own workspace where they can access relevant information, complete assigned tasks, manage requests, and interact with workplace processes.

Can managers manage their teams?

Yes. Managers can have visibility into their teams, assign tasks, review activity, and handle requests that require their attention.

Can WorkforceOS support multiple branches or departments?

Yes. WorkforceOS is designed around organizational structures such as departments, branches, teams, and role-based access.

How does WorkforceOS handle access and permissions?

Access can be structured around roles and responsibilities so people can work with the information relevant to them.

Can WorkforceOS work with our existing processes?

WorkforceOS can be configured around the workforce processes your organization needs.

The WorkforceOS team can walk you through how the platform fits your current setup during a demo.

How do I get started?

Book a demo with the WorkforceOS team. We’ll walk you through the platform and show you how WorkforceOS can fit into the way your organization works.
